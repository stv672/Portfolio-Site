Just a little
story about me.

Ever since I was a kid, I’ve been fascinated by technology. But as I grew older, I realized my mind was always filled with “a hundred thousand whys” about human behavior and everyday friction. I didn’t just want to build tech, I wanted to simplify the connection between people and devices.

That curiosity led me from Management Information Systems (MIS) straight into Product Design. I saw a unique gap where I could be more than just a designer, I could act as a true translator between user needs, business goals, and engineering teams.

I believe design is only as good as its execution. To test my limits, I stepped into competitions like Designathon Vietnam to validate my strategy under pressure. Thanks to my MIS and Quality Assurance background, I ensure every flow is not only intuitive but also feasible, testable, and handoff-ready.

When I’m not analyzing product flows or inspecting UI, you’ll find me playing the bass guitar.

How I Work

From messy requirements to usable product flows.

My strongest pattern is combining UX thinking with system logic and product quality. I care about whether the flow is understandable, feasible, consistent, and testable.

01 · Frame

Clarify the problem

Turn vague briefs, stakeholder needs, and technical constraints into a clear problem statement and product scope.

02 · Structure

Map the product logic

Define information architecture, funnel flow, core features, and edge cases before polishing screens.

03 · Design

Wireframe to interface

Create wireframes, UI states, visual hierarchy, and reusable component patterns that are easy to hand off.

04 · Validate

Test clarity and quality

Use feedback, QA thinking, and behaviour signals such as Hotjar/RUM to identify friction and improve product quality.
